Why Gashboard Is Different (Plain-English Summary):
Traditional hosting panels leave an admin login page open on the public internet (like port 8080 or 8443) for bots to attack. Gashboard does the opposite: the dashboard lives safely on your own computer (127.0.0.1 only) and connects to your servers exclusively over encrypted SSH keys pinned in your known_hosts file. No public control-panel ports, no exposed database ports, and 1-click rollbacks before every deployment.

2. Quickstart: Starting Gashboard Locally

  1. Start the Local Go SSH & DB Bridge (Terminal 1): 
    set GASHBOARD_BRIDGE_TOKEN=replace-with-random-secret
gashboard-ssh-windows-amd64.exe --listen 127.0.0.1:9391 --known-hosts %USERPROFILE%\.ssh\known_hosts --sqlite-root storage
  2. Initialize / Upgrade Local SQLite Vault & Start Gashboard (Terminal 2): 
    grab migrate --db-config grab.data.json --allow-db main:migrate migrations\003_dns_vault.grab
set GASHBOARD_MASTER_KEY=replace-with-strong-passphrase
set GASHBOARD_BRIDGE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:9391
grab serve --addr 127.0.0.1:9390 --allow-scripts --static public ^
  --db-config grab.data.json --allow-db main:read-write ^
  --platform-config grab.platform.json --allow-cache gashboard:read-write ^
  --allow-read . --allow-write storage ^
  --allow-env GASHBOARD_MASTER_KEY,GASHBOARD_BRIDGE_URL,GASHBOARD_BRIDGE_TOKEN,GASHBOARD_GRABTACULOUS_URL,GASHBOARD_GRABTACULOUS_TOKEN,GASHBOARD_UPSTREAM_READY_URL,GASHBOARD_HTTPS_PROBE_URL ^
  --allow-net 127.0.0.1,localhost src\gashboard.grab
  3. Open Your Private Browser Control Panel: Navigate to http://127.0.0.1:9390/gashboard.

3. Daily Operator Workflows

Configuring Namecheap DNS & Auto-HTTPS Without Breaking Email

  1. Step 1 — Save Encrypted Credentials: Enter your Namecheap API User, API Key, and Whitelisted Client IPv4 into Step 1 of the Namecheap DNS & Auto-HTTPS Wizard and click Save Encrypted Vault Credentials. Your browser wipes the raw key immediately and shows only a masked summary.
  2. Step 2 — Preview Email-Safe Changes: Pick your destination VPS node, enter your domain and subdomain (@ for apex or a prefix like shop), and click Preview Email-Safe DNS Changes. Review the plain-English table showing your MX, SPF, and DKIM email records marked as kept intact, then click Approve & Apply DNS Records.
  3. Step 3 & 4 — Propagation Guard & Auto-HTTPS: Click Preflight Propagation & Plan HTTPS. Until DNS resolvers return your target VPS IP, Gashboard blocks Let's Encrypt issuance (422 GASH-E422) to protect your rate limits. Once propagated (or in Cloudflare Tunnel mode), click Provision Auto-HTTPS & Verify /_ready. If you ever need to revert, click 1-Click Edge + DNS Rollback to restore both Grabtaculous and your prior Namecheap host records together.

Switching Light & Dark Themes

Click the Switch to Dark Theme / Switch to Light Theme button in the header. Gashboard stores the preference in SQLite and renders data-grex-theme server-side so refreshes apply your theme immediately with zero flash.

Browsing Databases Safely Without Exposing Public Ports

Pick a local confined SQLite file or open an encrypted 127.0.0.1 PostgreSQL/MariaDB SSH tunnel first, then choose that target in the Visual Database Manager panel. Use Load Schema to inspect columns, indexes, and foreign keys, paginate up to 100 rows per page, or run a read-only SELECT / EXPLAIN query.

To modify data or structure, click Preview & Create Pre-Mutation Snapshot first, verify the plain-English summary and SHA-256 snapshot digest, and then click Approve & Execute Write. If anything looks wrong afterward, click 1-Click Restore Snapshot to revert atomically.

Deploying & Rolling Back Site DNA

Paste a portable Groxy AI Site DNA JSON bundle, click Validate Site DNA & Lockfile Diff, inspect the preview card, and click Approve & Push Zero-Downtime Rollout. A single-use approval_token is consumed, a Pre-Deploy Snapshot is saved automatically, and 1-Click Rollback restores the previous known-good state with /_ready HTTP 200 verification.
